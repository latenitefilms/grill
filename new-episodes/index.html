New Episodes

You can find the latest episodes on YouTube.

FCG-228: BETTER FCP Multi-Cam - Fenwick

Description:

Chris demonstrates the multicam workflow behind the previous episode, using compound clips to organize camera angles, build a three-person layout, and manage color and audio. This companion tutorial shows how preparation can make the edit faster and more flexible.

FCG-228: Final Cut 13 Announcement - Brad Olsen & Dylan Bates

Description:

Brad Olsen and Dylan Bates join Chris for the Grill's first three-person conversation, discussing Apple's Final Cut announcement and what it could mean for editors. They also share plans for a Final Cut Pro Un-Conference.

FCG-227: Apple HATES FCP - Brad Olsen

Description:

Chris and Brad Olsen examine how Apple presents Final Cut Pro on its website, comparing its current visibility with earlier years and competing editing tools. They discuss their frustration with Apple's promotion of the application and what they would like to see change.

FCG-226: Choose the Right Ladder - Jeff Bates - “The Final Cut Dad”

Description:

Jeff Bates, Dylan Bates' father, joins Chris to talk about his filmmaking career, family, and the choices that shape a working life. Their conversation explores creative influences, collaboration, editing technology, and balancing work with home.

FCG-225: Why I edit in Final Cut Pro - James Branch

Description:

James Branch discusses his path into editing, his move to Final Cut Pro, and the detailed tutorials he shares on YouTube. Chris and James also explore AI, changing editing tools, and the experience of teaching and learning online.

FCG-224: Not Sucking Blood - Geoffrey Orthwein

Description:

Geoffrey Orthwein shares an early discussion of his new film and explains why he edited it in Final Cut Pro. The conversation covers post-production, color grading, storytelling, and the film's pandemic-related themes.

FCG-223: I Need New Conspiracies - John Davidson

Description:

John Davidson discusses making his documentary Epidemic of Fraud, seeking government records, and his claims about COVID-19 and media coverage. He also explains how he used Final Cut Pro to organize the material and tell the story.

FCG-222: Nostalgia and Video Production - Doc Rock

Description:

Doc Rock and Chris compare experiences from their long careers in video production, with detours into woodworking, technology, and community. Their discussion also covers Final Cut Pro, Resolve, audio workflows, and the editing tools they would like to improve.

FCG-221: Final Cut Pro and AI - Mark Spencer

Description:

Ripple Training's Mark Spencer joins Chris to discuss Final Cut Pro, Motion, and his growing interest in Claude and AI-assisted workflows. They explore 3D tools, automation, motion graphics, and how personal AI assistants might fit into creative work.

FCG-220: From Premiere to Final Cut for Reasons - Josh Laplap

Description:

After meeting on Threads, Chris and Josh Laplap talk about editing, moving between Premiere and Final Cut Pro, and the advantages of the magnetic timeline. They also discuss captions, plugins, Final Cut Pro 12.3, and features they would like Apple to add.

FCG-219: Using Riverside with Final Cut

Description:

Chris walks through his switch from OBS and Zoom to Riverside for recording the Grill. He demonstrates bringing the recordings into Final Cut Pro, building a multicam edit, aligning enhanced audio, and working around problems with exported chapter markers.

FCG-218: BTS Transition from FCP7 to X Inside Apple - Colleen Pendergast

Description:

Colleen Pendergast reflects on 16 years working on Final Cut at Apple, spanning the classic application and its transition to Final Cut Pro X. She and Chris discuss design decisions, collaboration inside Apple, and the challenges of bringing editing features to life.

FCG-217: Teaching Filmmaking - Cirina Catania

Description:

Cirina Catania joins Chris to talk about filmmaking, but their conversation begins with a look at how he produces the Grill. At Cirina's encouragement, Chris keeps that behind-the-scenes discussion, including his thoughts about moving the show away from Zoom.

FCG-216: BTS of Apple Retail - CJ Covell

Description:

CJ Covell and Chris explore life inside Apple retail, from store theaters and workshops to small-business support and the details of customer service. They also discuss Steve Jobs, working with an iPad, and the care that goes into the store experience.

FCG-214: Leaving the Apple Orchard - Wes Plate

Description:

Wes Plate talks about creating tools for Final Cut editors through Automatic Duck before joining Apple. He shares stories from his time inside the company and reflects on his career and departure.

FCG-213: FCP Needs Better Color Science - Jeff Greenberg

Description:

Jeff Greenberg returns for a discussion about color science and the capabilities Chris wants Apple to add to Final Cut Pro. The conversation revisits a topic that has repeatedly come up with other editors and guests.

FCG-212: Future of Final Cut Pro - Matthew O’Brien

Description:

Following an introduction from Dylan Bates, Chris sits down with Matthew O'Brien to learn more about his work and what motivates him. Final Cut Pro provides the starting point for a conversation that reaches beyond the editing application.

FCG-211: PhotoJoseph on FCP and Resolve

Description:

PhotoJoseph explains why he moved from Final Cut Pro to Resolve and why he prefers working with tracks. He and Chris discuss his background in photography, plans to revisit Final Cut, and how to make a useful comparison between the two applications.

Apple Releases Final Cut Pro 12.3

Description:

Chris and Dylan Bates get together for an impromptu conversation about the Final Cut Pro 12.3 release. They discuss the update, subtitles, and the response from creators.

FCG-210: Cinema Therapy and Final Cut Pro - Alan Seawright

Description:

Alan Seawright discusses the growth of Cinema Therapy, the YouTube channel he makes with Jono, and why Final Cut Pro has remained his editor of choice. He and Chris also consider why editors feel so strongly about the tools they use.

FCG-209: Resolve Editor Remembers Final Cut Pro - Tony Gallardo

Description:

Tony Gallardo looks back on his enthusiasm for Final Cut Pro and explains why he now works in DaVinci Resolve. Drawing on experience with both Apple and Blackmagic, he and Chris examine color tools, keyframing, and collaboration workflows.

FCG-208: Fun with Keyframe Toolbox - Iain Anderson

Description:

Iain Anderson guides Chris through an unrehearsed first attempt at using Keyframe Toolbox. Working through a simple animation, they explore the plugin's controls and show the learning process, including mistakes along the way.

FCG-207: Final Cut Pro: The Clock is Ticking - Rafael Ludwig

Description:

Rafael Ludwig explains what he enjoys about Final Cut Pro and why he also turns to other editing applications. He and Chris discuss its limitations, its future, and a prediction that leaves Chris unusually lost for words.

FCG-206: Final Cut - Extremely Grateful.. BUT - Brad Olsen

Description:

Brad Olsen joins Chris to discuss Final Cut Pro and the history of the people who built it. They also look back at Brad's documentary Off the Tracks and how listening to the original Grill podcast helped inspire the film.

FCG-205: Final Cut… Speed, Efficiency and Fun! - Dylan Bates

Description:

Dylan Bates talks with Chris about Final Cut Pro, Motion, and teaching editing techniques through clear, focused tutorials. They discuss his enthusiasm for the tools and his approach to recreating After Effects techniques in Motion.

FCG-204: Hey Apple… fix these things. - Jeff Greenberg

Description:

Jeff Greenberg and Chris discuss Final Cut Pro's color science, grouping in Resolve, and changes they would like Apple to make. The conversation also takes in fatherhood, social media, Reddit, and how many chapters a YouTube video really needs.
